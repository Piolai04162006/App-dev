# The Vampire Diaries 🧛

## About the Series
**The Vampire Diaries** is a *supernatural teen drama* that aired on **The CW** from **2009 to 2017**. It is based on the novel series by **L. J. Smith**.

> A story of love, loyalty, and family set in the small town of Mystic Falls, Virginia.

---

## Main Characters
1. **Elena Gilbert** – played by Nina Dobrev
2. **Stefan Salvatore** – played by Paul Wesley
3. **Damon Salvatore** – played by Ian Somerhalder

## Supernatural Beings in the Show
- Vampires
- Witches
- Werewolves
- Hybrids
  - *Originals* (the first vampires)
